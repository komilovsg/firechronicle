## What a hotspot is not
An active fire detection (hotspot) marks a pixel containing at least one fire at the moment of the satellite overpass. A detection count is not burned area and not fire size. Several detections can come from one large fire, and a small or short fire between overpasses can be missed.

## Things that hide or distort fires
Clouds and thick smoke can hide fires. Near-real-time months are provisional and are replaced when science-quality data arrive. Terra and Aqua orbits are drifting, which changes their overpass times. Suomi NPP stopped on 2 November 2026. Calibration ratios are fitted on 2012-2025 and held at their 2025 level for later years.

## Not a forecast
High-activity months are historical climatology. FireChronicle does not predict where or when a specific fire will start.

## Not an official product
FireChronicle uses NASA FIRMS data but is not an official NASA product and is not endorsed by NASA.
