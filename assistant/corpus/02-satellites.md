## Coverage dates in NASA FIRMS
MODIS Collection 6.1: 1 November 2000 to present.
VIIRS Suomi NPP (S-NPP) 375 m: 20 January 2012 to 2 November 2026.
VIIRS NOAA-20 375 m: 1 April 2018 to present.
VIIRS NOAA-21 375 m: 1 March 2023 to present.

## Suomi NPP end
Delivery of new science data from the Suomi NPP satellite ceased at 13:00 UTC on 2 November 2026, as announced by NOAA NESDIS and NASA Earthdata. Historical Suomi NPP data remain available. NASA FIRMS recommends NOAA-20 and NOAA-21 VIIRS as replacements: they carry an identical VIIRS sensor with the same 375 m resolution and fly in the same orbital plane as Suomi NPP, about 50 minutes apart.

## MODIS end of life
NASA Earthdata states that in late 2026 or early 2027 the Terra and Aqua platforms that carry MODIS will begin shutting down operations, and that both platforms are drifting out of their designed orbits, which affects crossing times.

## Overpass times
MODIS on Terra provides the only morning overpass, about 3 hours before Aqua. MODIS on Aqua has an afternoon overpass similar to Suomi NPP, NOAA-20 and NOAA-21.
